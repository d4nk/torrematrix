Questions we get

Straight answers about price, timing and who owns what.

How much does a website cost with Torre Matrix?

Plans are $99, $199 or $299 a month. Starter ($99) is your website, up to 5 pages, with 2 changes a month. Spotlight ($199) adds Google profile care, up to 8 pages and 8 changes a month. Full Service ($299) adds Instagram and Facebook management and 30 changes a month.

Do I have to pay anything up front?

No. You pay $0 at signing. Website plans run for 12 months at one monthly rate, and your price is locked for the whole term. Your .com, hosting and the security padlock are included.

How fast do you make changes to my site?

On Spotlight and Full Service, changes are done within 48 hours: 8 a month on Spotlight, 30 a month on Full Service. Starter includes 2 changes a month. One change is one small edit: a new price, new hours, a photo swap, or one line of text.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Cathedral City, CA and build websites for businesses across the Coachella Valley, including Palm Springs, Palm Desert, Rancho Mirage and Desert Hot Springs.

Can my website be in English and Spanish?

Yes. We build sites in English and Spanish, with one tap to switch the whole site. Las Adelitas Market's site is Spanish first, then English. Social media captions can be in English or bilingual.

Can you take care of my Google Business Profile and reviews?

Yes. Google profile care is 4 Google posts a month, replies to your reviews and fresh photos. It's included in Spotlight and Full Service, or $59 a month on its own, month to month. Setting up or cleaning up your Google listing is $99 one time, free with any package or with Google profile care. We never pay for reviews or reward them.

Do you manage Instagram and Facebook?

Yes. We post 3 times a week, answer comments and messages within 1 business day, and your pages stay in your name. It's $179 a month on its own, or included in Full Service with Stories for your specials and events.

Who will I work with, and who owns my .com?

You work directly with the owner, Tony De La Torre. Call or text (442) 234-2863, or email tony@torrematrix.com. We register your .com for you and move it into your name when your 12-month term ends. Your Facebook and Instagram pages stay in your name.
